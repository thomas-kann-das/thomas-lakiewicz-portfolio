Challenge

The brand of Sedlmayr Grund und Immobilien AG needed to be modernised — without losing its connection to the company's history. The goal was a carefully updated logo, an extended corporate design and a stronger employer brand.

Solution

Together with the management team I developed a brand credo and a brand circle. The logo was carefully modernised and the visual identity extended with a new typeface, additional colours and a consistent image style.
