profile.

hi — thank you for visiting my page.

If you like to know, here is a little bit about me: i am a communication designer based near Hamburg.

I've been visualizing digital and print communication for more than 15 years now — and I still get excited every time a new project starts. I love good typography, clean layouts and excited by the unexpected.

I've worked in both classic advertising and on the corporate side, so I've seen brands from pretty much every angle. What I love most is helping companies find and shape their identity — turning ideas into something real and meaningful.

I'm a big believer in good teamwork and enjoy exploring new directions together with my clients (and even better, in interdisciplinary teams). I'm persistent when it comes to getting great results — and nothing makes me happier than seeing clients genuinely excited about the outcome.

  Don't be shy and come in contact, I'm looking forward to get to know you.

selected work.

Five projects that needed a clear idea before they needed a logo.

KONKAV
Agency
Naming Brand Design UX / UI Presentation
★ Deutscher Agenturpreis 2024 — Best Naming & Best B2B Agency Presentation

K16 GmbH — Europe's largest B2B presentation agency — needed a standalone brand identity to enter the market under a new name. I developed the name KONKAV, a full visual identity and a B2B campaign. The concept: turning B2B bold — condensing complex business insights into impactful, beautiful communications.

collana IT
Brand Strategy Brand Architecture Brand Design

collana IT had grown to 11 companies through a buy-and-build strategy, each with its own disconnected identity. I developed a One-Brand Strategy with a clear brand hierarchy, a flexible pattern system, a new image world and a complete launch toolkit — creating a scalable, trust-building brand across all touchpoints.

Bühler
Services
Sub-brand Campaign Art Direction Templates
★ Swiss Excellence Award 2022 — Sonderpreis B2B  ·  Deutscher Agenturpreis 2021 & 2022

For Bühler Group — a global leader in processing solutions — I repositioned their service portfolio visually and strategically. I developed a sub-brand system that clearly separates services from hardware, built a digital brand campaign around three 3D reels showing frictionless production processes, and created scalable templates for the global team.

Three Moon
Investments
Brand Design UX / UI Design Presentation

A private equity company needed an identity that communicates trust, clarity and bold thinking — for an audience of C-suite executives, PE funds and co-investors. I built the full visual identity based on the brand values: courage, clarity, innovation and groundedness, delivered across a website, print collateral and investor presentations.

fingentia
Naming Brand Design UX / UI Design Presentation

A Fintech start-up needed a complete brand identity for market entry — from mission and vision to naming, corporate design, website and pitch deck. In a strategy workshop I developed the concept of the "digital summit climber". The result: a modular, professional identity that scales even with limited resources.
